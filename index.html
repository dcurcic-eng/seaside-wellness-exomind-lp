06 / Common questions

Quick answers, plain language.

Does it hurt? +

Most patients describe a soft, rhythmic tap on the scalp. There are no needles, no electric currents, and no sedation. Some doze off. Others read or scroll on their phone.

How many sessions until I notice change? +

Most patients report subtle shifts by session three. The recommended course is six sessions, scheduled within two to three weeks for best outcomes.

Is it covered by insurance? +

Most major plans cover Exomind for MDD. Our team verifies your benefits before your first visit, so there are no billing surprises.

Can I take Exomind alongside therapy or meds? +

Yes. Exomind is designed to complement, not replace, your existing care plan. We coordinate with your therapist or psychiatrist when you give us permission.

What if it isn't working for me? +

If you do not see improvement after the initial course, we re-evaluate together. Krista will discuss alternative or adjunct approaches, including referrals to specialists in our care network.
